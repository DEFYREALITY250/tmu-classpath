TMU First-Year Survival Guide: Room & Floor Quirks

1. The TRS "7th Floor" Illusion (TRS 1-xxx, 2-xxx, 3-xxx)

Ted Rogers School of Management (55 Dundas St W) sits on top of a commercial retail building (Best Buy / Canadian Tire). TRS Floor 1 (e.g., TRS 1-067) is actually the 7th Floor of the building! TRS 2-xxx is the 8th Floor, and TRS 3-xxx is the 9th Floor. Never wander the ground floor looking for 1-067!

2. Kerr Hall's 4-Wing Loop & Basement Trap (KHN, KHE, KHS, KHW)

Kerr Hall wraps around the Quad in a continuous square loop on Floors 1, 2, and 3. However, the Basement (0xx rooms) is NOT a full loop—it has dead-end walls between wings! Always navigate Kerr Hall on the 1st or 2nd floor loop until you are in the exact wing (N/E/S/W), then go up or down.

3. DSQ Classes are Inside the Cineplex Movie Theatre!

Rooms like DSQ 06 or DSQ 09 are real stadium movie auditoriums inside 10 Dundas St E (at Yonge & Dundas). Enter at street level or from Dundas Station underground, and take the long escalators up to the 3rd Floor Cineplex concourse.

4. ENG "LG" Rooms & POD / LIB Split Levels

In the George Vari Engineering Building (ENG), LG stands for Lower Ground (one level down via central stairs or elevator from the Gould/Church lobby). In POD (Jorgenson Hall), the 2nd and 3rd floors connect seamlessly into the Library (LIB) and across the indoor bridge into Kerr Hall West (KHW) 2nd floor.
